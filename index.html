The Listening Commons

Human-accountable scholarship for the AI era.

The Listening Commons is a living research commons for human-led, AI-assisted, human-AI co-developed, and AI-led human-curated scholarship.

Commons logic

Post openly. Validate carefully. Keep authors answerable.

Open posting

Posting a work is not the same as validating it. The commons allows ideas to enter before formal endorsement.

Human inhabitation

Submitters explain the work in their own words, disclose the AI role, verify what can be verified, and take responsibility.

Earned validation

Validation is earned through disclosure, critique, revision, verification, expert review, and time.
