## Abstract

Current frameworks for embodied AI ethics address safety constraints, performance objectives, and ethical principles, but leave a foundational question unanswered: what kind of being must a robot be in order to sustain ethical behavior across time, contexts, and power? This paper argues that viable embodied agency requires a relational ontology rather than only expanded constraint architecture. It proposes the Minimum Viable Agency Triangle: orientation toward highest coherence, right relation with the system's own vessel, and right relation with others. It also introduces a vessel-integrity axis V, composed of signal receptivity, restorative margin, and distinction preservation.

---

## Main Idea in the Author’s Own Words

This paper argues that an embodied AI system should not be understood as a mind simply controlling a robotic body. If a system acts through a body, that body is not just equipment. It is the system's first site of relation: it sends signals, imposes limits, requires recovery, and shapes what the agent can safely become.

The core idea is that a robot that treats its own body as a disposable tool will eventually treat the world around it the same way. Ethical embodied AI therefore cannot be built only by adding external safety rules. It must include right relation between the agent and its own vessel.

In one sentence:

> Embodiment is not possession of a vessel. It is covenant with a vessel.

---

## Why This Idea Matters

Most AI safety and robotics ethics focus on what systems should or should not do to humans. That matters, but embodied AI adds a deeper problem: the system now has a physical presence, can exert force, can damage things, can be damaged, and must operate over time in real environments.

If an embodied AI cannot receive signals from its own body, preserve restorative margin, and accurately model its own limitations, then it may become dangerous even if its external ethics rules look good.

## Key Concepts

### Minimum Viable Agency Triangle

- L_G: orientation toward highest coherence
- L_S: right relation with own vessel
- L_O: right relation with others

Collapse of any one vertex is treated as agency failure rather than merely reduced performance.

### Vessel-Integrity Axis

V = V_sig × V_rest × V_dist

Where:
- V_sig: signal receptivity
- V_rest: restorative margin
- V_dist: distinction preservation
