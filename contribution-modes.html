Contribution modes

Describe how the work came into being.

Contribution Mode is not a quality rating. It is a disclosure category.

No AI involvement

The work was produced without AI tools. The submitter still accepts responsibility for the submission's claims, sources, errors, and revisions.

This paper was produced without AI involvement. The author originated, drafted, revised, and verified the work.

Human-led, AI-assisted

The human originated and directed the inquiry. AI helped with brainstorming, drafting, critique, coding, literature exploration, formalization, editing, or refinement.

This paper was human-led and AI-assisted. AI tools were used for drafting support, conceptual critique, and editorial refinement.

Human-AI co-developed

The idea emerged through sustained dialogue. The human and AI contributions are intertwined and cannot be cleanly separated.

The central concepts developed iteratively through conversation, critique, and revision.

AI-led, human-curated

The AI generated the central idea, structure, model, or conjecture. A human reviewed it, judged it coherent or promising, disclosed the process, and submitted it for community evaluation.

The central idea was generated by an AI system. The human submitter reviewed it, found it coherent and promising, and is submitting it for community critique.

AI-generated, unverified

The work is mostly AI output and has not yet been meaningfully checked or inhabited by a human. It should not be presented as validated research.

This submission is AI-generated and largely unverified. It is posted as an exploratory candidate for review, not as established research.
