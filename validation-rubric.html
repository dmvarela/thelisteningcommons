Validation rubric

Posting is open. Validation is earned.

A submission may be posted openly, but validation is earned through reasoned community engagement, reference checking, expert review, revision, and time.

1. Research Program

Opens a durable line of inquiry and could become a field of work.

2. Real Research

Coherent, grounded, and worth serious scholarly engagement.

3. Promising Research Idea

There is something real here, but it needs development.

4. Needs Major Grounding

The paper has a possible insight, but its current form overreaches.

5. Technically Implausible

The mechanism, math, evidence, implementation, or feasibility claim does not currently hold.

6. AI Slop

The work appears fluent but empty, with little evidence of accountable human authorship.

Review dimensions

Reviewers should consider originality, coherence, grounding, technical plausibility, literature fit, human inhabitation, AI disclosure quality, usefulness, and revision potential.

Validation statuses

  • Unreviewed Working Paper
  • Community Commented
  • Reference Checked
  • Expert Reviewed
  • Revised After Critique
  • Challenged
  • Provisionally Validated
  • Superseded
  • Retracted
