## Section 5 — Contribution Mode

Choose one:

### No AI involvement
The work was produced without AI tools. The submitter still accepts responsibility for the submission's claims, sources, errors, and revisions.

### Human-led, AI-assisted
The human originated and directed the inquiry. AI helped with brainstorming, drafting, critique, coding, literature exploration, formalization, editing, or refinement.

### Human–AI co-developed
The idea emerged through sustained dialogue. The human and AI contributions are intertwined and cannot be cleanly separated.

### AI-led, human-curated
The AI generated the central idea, structure, model, or conjecture. A human reviewed it, judged it coherent or promising, disclosed the process, and submitted it for community evaluation.

### AI-generated, unverified
The work is mostly AI output and has not yet been meaningfully checked or inhabited by a human. This may be accepted only in a clearly marked sandbox or exploratory area, not as validated research.
