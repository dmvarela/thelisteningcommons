# Contribution Modes

The Listening Commons separates how a work was produced from how good it is.

Contribution Mode is not a quality rating. It is a disclosure category.

## Human-led, AI-assisted

The human originated and directed the inquiry. AI helped with brainstorming, drafting, critique, coding, literature exploration, formalization, editing, or refinement.

Example statement:

> This paper was human-led and AI-assisted. AI tools were used for drafting support, conceptual critique, and editorial refinement. The author selected the final arguments, verified the references, and takes responsibility for the submission.

## Human–AI co-developed

The idea emerged through sustained dialogue. The human and AI contributions are intertwined and cannot be cleanly separated.

Example statement:

> This paper emerged through sustained human–AI dialogue. The central concepts developed iteratively through conversation, critique, and revision. The human author reviewed, revised, selected the final claims, and takes responsibility for the work.

## AI-led, human-curated

The AI generated the central idea, structure, model, or conjecture. A human reviewed it, judged it coherent or promising, disclosed the process, and submitted it for community evaluation.

Example statement:

> The central idea was generated by an AI system. The human submitter reviewed it, found it coherent and promising, checked the initial claims where possible, and is submitting it for community critique rather than presenting it as validated research.

## AI-generated, unverified

The work is mostly AI output and has not yet been meaningfully checked or inhabited by a human.

This category may be allowed only in a clearly marked sandbox or exploratory area. It should not be presented as validated research.

Example statement:

> This submission is AI-generated and largely unverified. It is posted as an exploratory candidate for review, not as established research.

## Core Rule

AI-led is not disqualifying. Unaccountable is disqualifying.

A work can be AI-led and still promising.
A work can be human-led and still poor.
Validation depends on coherence, grounding, verification, and community critique.
