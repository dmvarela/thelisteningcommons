# The Listening Scale and Validation Rubric

The Listening Commons separates posting from validation.

A submission may be posted openly, but validation is earned through reasoned community engagement, reference checking, expert review, revision, and time.

## Listening Scale

### 1. Research Program

This work opens a durable line of inquiry. It suggests multiple papers, tests, models, applications, or debates. The idea is not merely interesting; it could become a field of work.

Indicators:
- strong original thesis
- multiple follow-up research paths
- coherent conceptual framework
- plausible links to existing literature
- could support future empirical, formal, or philosophical work

### 2. Real Research

The work is coherent, grounded, and worth serious scholarly engagement. It may still need revision, but it belongs in the research conversation.

Indicators:
- clear research question
- credible argument
- real references
- appropriate scope
- meaningful contribution

### 3. Promising Research Idea

There is something real here, but it needs development. The idea may require stronger definitions, better literature, clearer methods, technical refinement, or more evidence.

Indicators:
- interesting seed
- incomplete grounding
- early formalization
- needs sharper scope
- worth revising rather than discarding

### 4. Needs Major Grounding

The paper has a possible insight, but its current form overreaches. It needs better support, clearer claims, verified sources, stronger reasoning, or a narrower scope.

Indicators:
- broad claims unsupported
- insufficient literature
- unclear definitions
- promising but unstable
- overstates results

### 5. Technically Implausible

The idea is interesting, but the mechanism, math, evidence, implementation, or feasibility claim does not currently hold.

Indicators:
- mathematical issue
- empirical impossibility
- mechanism unsupported
- engineering feasibility absent
- strong claim with weak pathway

### 6. AI Slop

The work appears fluent but empty. It may contain weak understanding, fake depth, hallucinated citations, unsupported claims, or little evidence of accountable human authorship.

Indicators:
- author cannot explain the idea
- citations are fake or irrelevant
- claims are generic
- no coherent argument
- obvious AI artifacts
- polished language hiding conceptual emptiness

## Review Dimensions

Reviewers should not only vote. They should explain.

Suggested dimensions:

- Originality
- Coherence
- Grounding
- Technical plausibility
- Literature fit
- Human inhabitation
- AI disclosure quality
- Usefulness
- Revision potential

## Required Review Prompts

Every review should answer at least two:

1. What is the strongest part of this work?
2. What breaks or needs work?
3. Are the citations credible?
4. Does the author’s own-word explanation show understanding?
5. What would make this stronger?
6. Is this a real research path, or only a polished surface?

## Validation Statuses

### Unreviewed Working Paper
Posted but not yet meaningfully evaluated.

### Community Commented
Readers have engaged with the work.

### Reference Checked
Citations and quotations have been reviewed for accuracy.

### Expert Reviewed
At least one domain expert has evaluated the work.

### Revised After Critique
The author has responded to feedback and improved the work.

### Challenged
Significant unresolved objections exist.

### Provisionally Validated
The work has survived meaningful critique and is considered coherent, grounded, and worth citing.

### Superseded
A later version, correction, or better framework has replaced the current form.

### Retracted
The author or community has identified a serious flaw requiring withdrawal.
